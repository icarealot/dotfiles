Use [discussing](../discussing/SKILL.md) for the interview and [domain-modeling](../domain-modeling/SKILL.md) to capture settled terminology and decisions. During discussion, limit edits to glossary entries and ADRs.

When the open design decisions are resolved, summarize the agreed design and ask the user to confirm it. Begin implementation only after the user confirms the design and explicitly asks you to implement it.
